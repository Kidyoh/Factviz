Type any question about a size, distance, date or process and get an animated, to-scale picture of the real figures, with a source for every number.

## What's in it

- **20 visualization types**: lifelike size scenes (a person, a newborn giraffe and an adult giraffe standing side by side), planets on a starfield, timelines, exponential growth, comparisons, step-by-step processes, donuts, waffles, treemaps, bubbles, rankings, number lines, before/after, ranges, head-to-head, live counters, line charts, a "history in one day" clock and world maps.
- **131 pictures**: animals, dinosaurs, people, landmarks, vehicles and everyday objects, drawn to scale.
- **A library of 114 ready-made visualizations** in ten categories (space, Earth, animals, the body, food, history, people, science, technology, records). It works offline and needs no key.
- **Ask your own questions** with your own OpenRouter key (tap the key icon on the home screen). The key is kept in Android's encrypted storage and only sent to OpenRouter.
- Share links that re-open a visualization without asking the AI again.

## Install

Download **factviz-v1.0.0-universal.apk** (works on any Android phone), or the smaller **arm64-v8a** build for most phones from the last few years. Open it on your phone and allow installing from your browser or file manager when asked.

If you installed a test build before, uninstall it first: test builds were signed with a different key.
